# Cape Canaveral on $0: A Free Space Coast Day

Every stop below is **free admission and free parking** (verified September 25, 2026 - sources at the bottom). Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex is great but paid (~$75+/person), so it's not on this list. This plan works any day it's not a Monday (see the day-of-week notes).

The area is spread out, so the day runs north to south: **Titusville in the morning, Cape Canaveral mid-day, Cocoa Beach for the beach and dinner.** Total driving: about 1 hour split into three short legs.

## Morning: Titusville

### Optional breakfast (8:30-9:00)
- **Downtown Diner** - 605 S Hopkins Ave, Titusville. Classic local diner, breakfast and lunch, open 7 AM-2 PM daily.

### Space View Park + Space Walk of Fame (9:00-11:30) - FREE
- **8 Broad St, Titusville, FL 32796** (riverfront, at US-1 and Orange St)
- Hours: roughly dawn to dusk (listed 7 AM-7 PM); admission and parking free.
- The best free NASA-adjacent stop on the Space Coast. You stand directly across the Indian River from Kennedy Space Center - clear sightline to the Vehicle Assembly Building and the launch pads, about 11-12 miles away.
- The park doubles as the **Space Walk of Fame**: outdoor monuments and plaques for Mercury, Gemini, Apollo, and Shuttle, plus an astronaut handprint display. All outdoors, all free.
- This is also one of the most popular free launch-viewing spots in Florida, so on a launch day it fills up hours early (see launch notes at the bottom).

### Lunch in downtown Titusville (11:45-1:00)
The park sits right at the edge of downtown - every option below is a 2-5 minute drive.
- **Shiloh's Steak & Seafood** - 3665 S Washington Ave, on the river with a view back toward KSC. Note the hours: lunch Friday-Sunday from 11 AM; Tuesday-Thursday it opens at 4 PM (closed Monday).
- **Dixie Crossroads** - 1475 Garden St. Space Coast institution since 1983, famous for rock shrimp and corn fritters. Open 11 AM-9 PM daily, so it works any day.
- **Downtown Diner** - 605 S Hopkins Ave, if you're in a breakfast-food mood (open until 2 PM).

## Midday: Cape Canaveral

**Drive (1:00-1:30):** Titusville to Cape Canaveral is about 25-30 minutes straight down US-1 / A1A.

### Sands Space History Center (1:30-3:00) - FREE
- **100 Space Port Way (Building 90328), Cape Canaveral, FL 32920**
- Hours: Tue-Fri 9 AM-4 PM, Sat 9 AM-5 PM, Sun 12-4 PM. **Closed Monday.**
- Admission and parking free; no base pass or tour needed because it sits just outside the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station south gate.
- A compact, genuinely good museum covering every launch complex at the Cape - the Mercury/Redstone era through today, with hardware, blockhouse gear, and launch photos. Gift shop, restrooms, and a shaded picnic area on site.
- (The rest of the Cape Canaveral Space Force Museum - the blockhouses and hangars inside the base - is only reachable by scheduled tour, so this center is the free, walk-in part.)

### Manatee Sanctuary Park (3:10-3:45) - FREE
- **701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral, FL 32920** (5 minutes from the Sands Center)
- Open 7 AM to dusk daily; admission and parking free.
- A small riverside park on the Banana River with a boardwalk and covered lookout. Manatees hang out in the warm shallows here - they're most reliable in the cooler months (roughly November-March), and on hot summer afternoons you may see none. Either way it's a quiet 30-minute leg-stretcher with a playground if kids are along.

## Afternoon: the beach

**Drive (3:45-4:00):** 10-12 minutes south on A1A into Cocoa Beach.

### Lori Wilson Park beach block (4:00-6:15) - FREE
- **1500 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach, FL 32931**
- Sunrise to dusk; admission and parking both free (unusual for an oceanfront park this size - the neighboring Cocoa Beach city parks charge for parking).
- 32 acres with a wide, lifeguarded beach (lifeguards seasonal), restrooms, outdoor showers, and a long boardwalk through a maritime hammock. This is the "ample beach time" block: swim, walk, watch surfers, or just park a chair.
- **Free-parking beach alternative closer to the Sands Center:** Cherie Down Park, 8330 Ridgewood Ave, Cape Canaveral - free parking, restrooms, showers. Use it if you'd rather not drive the extra 10 minutes, but Lori Wilson puts you closer to dinner.
- Optional 5-minute add-on on the way to dinner: **Ron Jon Surf Shop**, 4151 N Atlantic Ave (A1A at SR-520). The famous flagship store, free to wander, open 9 AM-11 PM daily.

## Evening: Cocoa Beach dinner (6:30+)

The beach at Lori Wilson is 5-10 minutes from all of these:
- **Florida's Seafood Bar & Grill** - 480 W Cocoa Beach Causeway. Local favorite for fresh catch; Mon-Thu 11:30 AM-9 PM, Fri-Sat until 9:30 PM, Sun 12-9 PM.
- **Coconuts on the Beach** - 2 Minutemen Cswy. Literally on the sand downtown; you can go straight from beach to table.
- **The Fat Snook** - 2464 S Atlantic Ave. Beachside spot for fresh local seafood and craft beer, a little more upscale-casual.
- **Gregory's Steak & Seafood Grille** - 900 N Atlantic Ave. Long-running family steak-and-seafood house if you want a sit-down dinner.

## Day-of-week and seasonal notes
- **Monday is the bad day:** the Sands Space History Center is closed, Shiloh's is closed, and several restaurants run short hours. Any other day works; Friday-Sunday gives you every option.
- **Manatees:** most reliable November-March at Manatee Sanctuary Park.
- **Lifeguards at Lori Wilson Park are seasonal** - swim accordingly off-season.

## If there's a launch
Space View Park is a prime free viewing spot and the whole area gets crowded. If your day lands on a launch, flip the plan: claim a spot at Space View Park 2-3 hours before liftoff and treat the beach as the morning block instead. Check the schedule the night before at spacelaunchschedule.com.

## Nearby spots deliberately left off (they charge)
- **Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex** - paid admission (~$75+).
- **Canaveral National Seashore / Playalinda Beach** - $15-25 per vehicle entrance fee.
- **Jetty Park (Port Canaveral)** - requires a pre-purchased paid parking pass.
- **American Space Museum, Titusville** - small paid admission (~$10); worth it if you're willing to spend, and it's a 4-block walk from Space View Park.
- **Exploration Tower, Port Canaveral** - paid admission.
