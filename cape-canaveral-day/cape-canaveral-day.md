# Cape Canaveral on $0: A Free Space Coast Day - Saturday, November 28, 2026

This plan is pinned to **Saturday, November 28, 2026 (Thanksgiving weekend)**. Every attraction below is **free admission and free parking** (verified September 25, 2026 - sources at the bottom). Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex is great but paid (~$75+/person), so it's not on this list.

**Start at 11 AM at the first site.** Sunset that day is **5:25 PM EST**, so the whole day is sequenced around it: the Cape Canaveral and Cocoa Beach stops first, a full beach block mid-afternoon, then one northbound drive to Titusville timed to walk Space View Park at sunset, and dinner there after.

## Late morning: Cape Canaveral

### Sands Space History Center (11:00-12:00) - FREE
- **100 Space Port Way (Building 90328), Cape Canaveral, FL 32920**
- Saturday hours: **9 AM-5 PM** (Tue-Fri 9-4, Sun 12-4, closed Monday). A Saturday 11 AM arrival sits comfortably inside that window.
- Admission and parking free; no base pass or tour needed because it sits just outside the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station south gate.
- The best free space-history stop near the Cape: a compact, genuinely good museum covering every launch complex - the Mercury/Redstone era through today, with hardware, blockhouse gear, and launch photos. Gift shop, restrooms, and a shaded picnic area on site.
- (The rest of the Cape Canaveral Space Force Museum - the blockhouses and hangars inside the base - is only reachable by scheduled tour, so this center is the free, walk-in part.)

### Manatee Sanctuary Park (12:10-12:45) - FREE
- **701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral, FL 32920** (5 minutes from the Sands Center)
- Open 7 AM to dusk daily; admission and parking free.
- A small riverside park on the Banana River with a boardwalk and covered lookout. **Late November is the start of prime manatee season (roughly November-March)**, when manatees gather in the warm shallows, so this is a good time of year to spot them - still not a guarantee on any given afternoon, but the odds are as good as they get.

## Lunch: Cocoa Beach (12:55-2:05)

Manatee Sanctuary Park is about 10 minutes from the Cocoa Beach restaurant corridor, so the day rolls straight from the last stop into lunch. It's the Saturday of Thanksgiving weekend, so expect a wait at the most popular spots around 1 PM; the options below spread you out along the causeway and A1A.
- **Florida's Seafood Bar & Grill** - 480 W Cocoa Beach Causeway. Local favorite for fresh catch; Mon-Thu 11:30 AM-9 PM, Fri-Sat until 9:30 PM, Sun from 12 PM.
- **Coconuts on the Beach** - 2 Minutemen Cswy. Literally on the sand downtown; beachy lunch with a view. Downtown will be busiest this weekend (the annual Cocoa Beach Art Show runs downtown over Thanksgiving weekend).
- **Gregory's Steak & Seafood Grille** - 900 N Atlantic Ave. Long-running family steak-and-seafood house, usually the easiest sit-down table when downtown is slammed.

## Afternoon: the beach

**Drive (2:05-2:15):** 5-10 minutes depending on where you ate.

### Lori Wilson Park beach block (2:15-4:15) - FREE
- **1500 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach, FL 32931**
- Sunrise to dusk; admission and parking both free (unusual for an oceanfront park this size - the neighboring Cocoa Beach city parks charge for parking).
- 32 acres with a wide beach, restrooms, outdoor showers, and a long boardwalk through a maritime hammock. Two full hours here: swim, walk, watch surfers, or just park a chair.
- **Late-November reality check:** water is cool (low 70s F) and **lifeguards are seasonal, so expect no lifeguard on duty** - swim accordingly. Crowds are moderate over Thanksgiving weekend, and this park sits about 2 miles south of the downtown art-show bustle, so parking is far easier than at the downtown accesses.
- **Free-parking beach alternative closer to the morning stops:** Cherie Down Park, 8330 Ridgewood Ave, Cape Canaveral - free parking, restrooms, showers. Use it if you'd rather skip the extra drive south, but Lori Wilson has the longer, better-serviced beach block.
- Optional 5-minute add-on only if you leave the beach a few minutes early: **Ron Jon Surf Shop**, 4151 N Atlantic Ave (A1A at SR-520). The famous flagship store, free to wander, open 9 AM-11 PM daily.

## Evening: Titusville sunset, then dinner

**Drive (4:15-5:00):** Lori Wilson Park to Space View Park is about 29 miles, roughly 40 minutes; on a holiday-weekend Saturday, budget 45-50 minutes and leave by 4:15. Go north just once. [Space View Park map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Space+View+Park&query_place_id=ChIJbSHkZOqz4IgRjnwVudA8730).

### Space View Park and U.S. Space Walk of Fame (5:00-5:35) - FREE
- **8 Broad St, Titusville, FL 32796** (city listing; map pins it at 10 Broad St). Free admission and limited free parking. Try the lot at the end of Broad Street.
- The outdoor Mercury, Gemini, Apollo and Shuttle memorials are the **U.S. Space Walk of Fame**, within the park, not a separate drive or ticket. The nearby American Space Museum charges admission and is not part of this stop.
- **Sunset on November 28 is 5:25 PM EST** (civil twilight ends about 5:51). The park sits on the west bank of the Indian River facing the Kennedy Space Center pads, so arrive by 5:00, walk the memorials as the light drops, and catch the sun setting across the river toward the pads. Park hours are sunrise to sunset for an ordinary visit, and this slot wraps right at closing - don't plan to linger after 5:35.

### Dinner in Titusville (5:45-7:00)

All three below are **confirmed open on Saturdays** (checked September 25, 2026), with both meat and substantial vegetarian mains; Google Maps ratings as of that date. They are paid meals, not free attractions. **The Saturday after Thanksgiving is a busy restaurant weekend: for Cafe Paradiso (a small room) call a day or two ahead; Indian Sizzler and Third Culture Kitchen are larger and walk-in friendly.** Holiday-weekend hours can shift, so do a quick same-day confirmation call.

1. **[Cafe Paradiso Bistro & Social Club](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+Paradiso+Bistro+%26+Social+Club&query_place_id=ChIJWdyi-Waz4IgRyOID715_2mM)** - 337 S Washington Ave, a short hop from the park. **4.7/5 (730 ratings).** Creole/Cajun chicken, pork and seafood, plus meatless tofu versions of entrees and a vegan portobello steak on its specialty menu. **Open Tue-Sat (Fri-Sat to ~11 PM); closed Monday; Sunday is brunch only until 3 PM.** The Saturday pick if you book ahead.
2. **[Indian Sizzler](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Indian+Sizzler&query_place_id=ChIJX74ozTyz4IgRgRyZRw3JB4U)** - 3520 S Washington Ave, about 10 minutes south of the park. **4.7/5 (1,335 ratings).** Butter chicken and chicken tikka masala for meat eaters; palak paneer, dal makhani, vegetable biryani and vegetable vindaloo among numerous vegetarian mains. **Tue-Sun 11 AM-9 PM; closed Monday.**
3. **[Third Culture Kitchen](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Third+Culture+Kitchen&query_place_id=ChIJq6qaO1JN54gRjcSUsEwZlaY)** - 1000 Cheney Hwy, a short drive west. **4.5/5 (2,060 ratings).** Brisket/chicken tacos and Korean fried chicken alongside a vegan tofu coconut bowl, vegan burrito and avocado tacos. **Daily: Mon-Thu/Sun 11 AM-9 PM; Fri-Sat 11 AM-10 PM.** The fallback that never closes for the weekend.

**Checked but not selected:** [Dixie Crossroads](https://dixiecrossroads.com/directions) serves dinner daily 11 AM-9 PM (except Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day - fine for this Saturday), but its menu did not establish substantial vegetarian mains. Shiloh's Steak & Seafood **closed permanently in January 2026**, despite stale directory listings. Downtown Diner closes at 2 PM and serves only breakfast/lunch. If a day-of closure disrupts Titusville dinner, eat in Cocoa Beach instead.

## If the date moves
- **Monday:** Sands, Cafe Paradiso and Indian Sizzler are closed; Third Culture Kitchen serves dinner daily.
- **Sunday:** Sands opens at noon, so do Manatee Sanctuary Park first; Cafe Paradiso is brunch-only, so pick Indian Sizzler or Third Culture Kitchen for dinner.

## If there's a launch
A Falcon Heavy carrying Astrobotic's Griffin Mission One is on the manifest from LC-39A, but **no November 28 or 29 launch date or 7:00 PM liftoff time is confirmed**. [Next Spaceflight](https://nextspaceflight.com/launches/details/6759/) currently says only **NET November 2026**; [Astrobotic's own manifest](https://www.astrobotic.com/lunar-delivery/manifest/) says **2026**. Some trackers convert an approximate November 30, 00:00 UTC database value into Sunday, November 29 at 7:00 PM EST; [another tracker explicitly flags the timestamp as tentative rather than a confirmed window](https://spacelaunchlive.com/launches/falcon-heavy-griffin-mission-one/). Check [SpaceX updates](https://www.spacex.com/launches/) and a live tracker the night before and again that morning.
- If a real window lands **Saturday**, rebuild the beach and Titusville timing around it, rather than treating this sunset plan as launch viewing. Space View Park is a free riverfront viewing spot with limited free parking; confirm any after-dark launch access with the city.
- If an **evening window is confirmed for Sunday, November 29**, use the [companion Sunday launch-watch itinerary](https://github.com/peshzero/filedrop/blob/main/cape-canaveral-day/cape-canaveral-sunday-launch.md). That plan is conditional and includes a no-launch alternative. Launches slip or scrub, so keep the rest of the trip worth doing without one.

## Why the route is shaped this way
Everything south of Titusville runs in one line (Cape Canaveral to Cocoa Beach), then a single 29-mile northbound drive lands the riverfront memorials right at sunset and dinner five minutes later. Space View Park's sunrise-to-sunset hours are the constraint that sets the beach's 4:15 PM departure - moving the park any later puts the visit in the dark.
