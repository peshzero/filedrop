# Cape Canaveral on $0: A Free Space Coast Day (11 AM Start)

Every stop below is **free admission and free parking** (verified September 25, 2026 - sources at the bottom). Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex is great but paid (~$75+/person), so it's not on this list.

**Start at 11 AM at the first site.** Keep the Cape Canaveral / Cocoa Beach stops together through the beach, then make one northbound trip to Titusville for a riverfront walk and dinner. The 29-mile drive from Lori Wilson Park takes about 40 minutes; budget 40-50 minutes, more on launch days. Space View Park must be visited before sunset, so shift the afternoon earlier on short days.

**Monday:** Sands is closed. Start at Manatee Sanctuary Park at 11, extend lunch and beach, and keep Titusville for the evening; do not drive north twice.

## Late morning: Cape Canaveral

### Sands Space History Center (11:00-12:15) - FREE
- **100 Space Port Way (Building 90328), Cape Canaveral, FL 32920**
- Hours: Tue-Fri 9 AM-4 PM, Sat 9 AM-5 PM, Sun 12-4 PM. **Closed Monday.**
- Admission and parking free; no base pass or tour needed because it sits just outside the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station south gate.
- The best free space-history stop near the Cape: a compact, genuinely good museum covering every launch complex - the Mercury/Redstone era through today, with hardware, blockhouse gear, and launch photos. Gift shop, restrooms, and a shaded picnic area on site.
- (The rest of the Cape Canaveral Space Force Museum - the blockhouses and hangars inside the base - is only reachable by scheduled tour, so this center is the free, walk-in part.)

### Manatee Sanctuary Park (12:25-1:00) - FREE
- **701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral, FL 32920** (5 minutes from the Sands Center)
- Open 7 AM to dusk daily; admission and parking free.
- A small riverside park on the Banana River with a boardwalk and covered lookout. Manatees hang out in the warm shallows - most reliable in the cooler months (roughly November-March), and on hot afternoons you may see none. Either way it's a quiet 30-minute leg-stretcher with a playground if kids are along.

## Lunch: Cocoa Beach (1:10-2:20)

Manatee Sanctuary Park is about 10 minutes from the Cocoa Beach restaurant corridor, so the day rolls straight from the last stop into lunch.
- **Florida's Seafood Bar & Grill** - 480 W Cocoa Beach Causeway. Local favorite for fresh catch; Mon-Thu 11:30 AM-9 PM, Fri-Sat until 9:30 PM, Sun from 12 PM. Works every day, including the Sunday flip.
- **Coconuts on the Beach** - 2 Minutemen Cswy. Literally on the sand downtown; beachy lunch with a view.
- **Gregory's Steak & Seafood Grille** - 900 N Atlantic Ave. Long-running family steak-and-seafood house if you want a sit-down lunch.

## Afternoon: the beach

**Drive (2:20-2:35):** 5-10 minutes depending on where you ate.

### Lori Wilson Park beach block (2:35-5:00) - FREE
- **1500 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach, FL 32931**
- Sunrise to dusk; admission and parking both free (unusual for an oceanfront park this size - the neighboring Cocoa Beach city parks charge for parking).
- 32 acres with a wide, lifeguarded beach (lifeguards seasonal), restrooms, outdoor showers, and a long boardwalk through a maritime hammock. This is the "ample beach time" block: swim, walk, watch surfers, or just park a chair for the afternoon.
- **Free-parking beach alternative closer to the morning stops:** Cherie Down Park, 8330 Ridgewood Ave, Cape Canaveral - free parking, restrooms, showers. Use it if you'd rather skip the extra drive south, but Lori Wilson has a long, well-serviced beach block before the northbound drive.
- Optional 5-minute add-on only if daylight allows: **Ron Jon Surf Shop**, 4151 N Atlantic Ave (A1A at SR-520). The famous flagship store, free to wander, open 9 AM-11 PM daily.

## Evening: Titusville riverfront, then dinner

**Drive (5:00-5:50):** Lori Wilson Park to Space View Park is about 29 miles, roughly 40 minutes; budget 40-50 minutes including traffic. Go north just once. [Space View Park map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Space+View+Park&query_place_id=ChIJbSHkZOqz4IgRjnwVudA8730).

### Space View Park and U.S. Space Walk of Fame (5:50-6:25, daylight only) - FREE
- **8 Broad St, Titusville, FL 32796** (city listing; map pins it at 10 Broad St). Free admission and limited free parking. Try the lot at the end of Broad Street.
- The outdoor Mercury, Gemini, Apollo and Shuttle memorials are the **U.S. Space Walk of Fame**, within the park, not a separate drive or ticket. See the riverfront view toward the Kennedy Space Center launch pads. Allow 30-40 minutes. The nearby American Space Museum charges admission and is not part of this stop.
- **Park hours: sunrise to sunset** for an ordinary visit; special after-dark launch viewing is an exception. The example 5:50-6:25 slot works only when sunset is after 6:25. On short winter days, leave the beach about **4:00-4:15**, reach Titusville about **4:50-5:05**, walk 25-35 minutes, and have dinner around **5:45**. To keep a full two hours at the beach, shorten the morning stops and move lunch/beach earlier. Check sunset for the actual date.

### Titusville dinner (about 6:35-7:50; earlier on short days)

The following are **three of the highest-rated, well-reviewed options found with both meat and substantial vegetarian mains** have Google Maps ratings checked September 25, 2026 (ratings change). They are paid meals, not free attractions. A rating tie favors proximity and dinner fit.

1. **[Café Paradiso Bistro & Social Club](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Caf%C3%A9+Paradiso+Bistro+%26+Social+Club&query_place_id=ChIJWdyi-Waz4IgRyOID715_2mM)** - 337 S Washington Ave, a short hop from the park. **4.7/5 (730 ratings).** Creole/Cajun chicken, pork and seafood, plus meatless tofu versions of entrées and a vegan portobello steak on its specialty menu. **Dinner Tuesday-Saturday**; closed Monday and **Sunday is brunch only until 3 PM**. Check the current menu and day's kitchen close time.
2. **[Indian Sizzler](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Indian+Sizzler&query_place_id=ChIJX74ozTyz4IgRgRyZRw3JB4U)** - 3520 S Washington Ave, about 10 minutes south of the park. **4.7/5 (1,335 ratings).** Butter chicken and chicken tikka masala for meat eaters; palak paneer, dal makhani, vegetable biryani and vegetable vindaloo among numerous vegetarian mains. Listed **Tuesday-Sunday to around 9 PM; closed Monday**. Confirm same-day dinner hours.
3. **[Third Culture Kitchen](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Third+Culture+Kitchen&query_place_id=ChIJq6qaO1JN54gRjcSUsEwZlaY)** - 1000 Cheney Hwy, a short drive west. **4.5/5 (2,060 ratings).** Brisket/chicken tacos and Korean fried chicken alongside a vegan tofu coconut bowl, vegan burrito and avocado tacos. **Daily: Mon-Thu/Sun 11 AM-9 PM; Fri-Sat 11 AM-10 PM.** The reliable Monday and Sunday choice.

**Checked but not selected:** [Dixie Crossroads](https://dixiecrossroads.com/directions) serves dinner daily 11 AM-9 PM (except Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day), but its menu did not establish substantial vegetarian mains. Shiloh's Steak & Seafood **closed permanently in January 2026**, despite stale directory listings. Downtown Diner closes at 2 PM and serves only breakfast/lunch. If a day-of closure disrupts Titusville dinner, eat in Cocoa Beach instead; do not depend on Shiloh's or the diner.

## Day-of-week and seasonal notes
- **Monday:** Sands, Café Paradiso and Indian Sizzler are closed. Manatee Sanctuary Park at 11, then lunch/beach and the Titusville park before sunset; Third Culture Kitchen serves dinner daily.
- **Sunday:** Sands opens at noon, so do Manatee Sanctuary Park first. Florida's Seafood opens at noon for the ~1:30 lunch. Café Paradiso closes after brunch; Indian Sizzler or Third Culture Kitchen are dinner options.
- **Manatees:** most reliable November-March at Manatee Sanctuary Park.
- **Lifeguards at Lori Wilson Park are seasonal** - swim accordingly off-season.

## If there's a launch
The whole area gets crowded on launch days, and the Cape Canaveral / Cocoa Beach corridor gives you decent free views right from the beach. The classic free viewing spot is Space View Park in Titusville (direct riverfront sightline to the pads, 30 min north) - if the launch is the priority, build the day around the actual liftoff: claim a spot 2-3 hours beforehand, moving or dropping beach as needed. Night launches can allow after-dark viewing, but do not assume the park is normally open at night. Check the schedule and road restrictions the night before at spacelaunchschedule.com.

## Why the route changed
Titusville is back **after** the beach because good mixed-diet dinners are available there. This adds one 29-mile northbound drive without breaking up lunch and beach. Space View Park and its outdoor Space Walk memorials form one free stop; the paid American Space Museum stays out.

## Nearby spots deliberately left off (they charge)
- **Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex** - paid admission (~$75+).
- **Canaveral National Seashore / Playalinda Beach** - $15-25 per vehicle entrance fee.
- **Jetty Park (Port Canaveral)** - requires a pre-purchased paid parking pass.
- **American Space Museum, Titusville** - small paid admission (~$10).
- **Exploration Tower, Port Canaveral** - paid admission.
