# Cape Canaveral Sunday morning, November 29, 2026: pre-noon launch watch or a short morning before departure

**You leave by noon on Sunday, November 29.** The first version of this file planned a full 11 AM - 7 PM launch-watch day; that day is impossible with a noon departure, and everything post-noon in it does NOT apply to you. This file now covers only what fits before noon:

- **Scenario B:** no morning launch - a short pre-departure morning: sunrise at the beach, a quick breakfast, and an optional Manatee Sanctuary Park stop, timed to be on the road by noon.

## Scenario A: a confirmed Sunday-MORNING window

A morning liftoff is the only launch that fits this trip, and it has one advantage over the placeholder 7 PM time: Space View Park is open dawn to dusk, so a morning watch sits inside **ordinary park hours** - no special after-dark launch access to confirm.

- **Space View Park / U.S. Space Walk of Fame**, 8 Broad St, Titusville. Free admission, limited free parking, unobstructed shoreline facing the Kennedy Space Center pads; the [city recommends it for launch watching](https://www.titusville.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=319). About 29 miles north of Cocoa Beach.
- **Arrive 60-90 minutes before liftoff.** For a Falcon Heavy on Thanksgiving weekend, treat 90 as the floor; the small riverside lots fill early. Bring a layer, water, a charged phone and power bank, and a downloaded webcast or live countdown - cell service gets patchy in a crowd.
- **The noon rule decides which windows are watchable.** A liftoff at roughly **10:30 AM or earlier** works: watch, then be rolling within 15-20 minutes of liftoff, ahead of the post-launch traffic crawl. A window near or after ~11 AM does not work: even a short hold pushes it past your departure, and launch holds of an hour or more are routine. If the announced window is marginal, the honest choice is to skip the watch rather than gamble the drive home on a countdown.
- **If the launch scrubs or slips past mid-morning,** fall back to Scenario B (or just leave). Do not wait around Titusville for a new time that eats the afternoon.

## Scenario B: no morning launch - a short pre-departure morning

All stops are free admission and free parking; breakfast is the only cost. The timing keeps a buffer so you are on the road **by 11:30 at the latest**, ahead of the noon hard limit.

| 7:45-8:45 | Breakfast: Southern Charm Cafe, Cape Canaveral | Meal |

**Cherie Down Park**, 8330 Ridgewood Ave, Cape Canaveral. Free parking, restrooms and showers; the closest free-parking ocean sunrise to the Cape. Sunrise is about **6:55 AM**, so arrive by 6:45 for first color. Late-November mornings are cool - bring a layer.

**Southern Charm Cafe**, 8501 Astronaut Blvd, Cape Canaveral. [Listed Sunday hours 6:30 AM-2:30 PM](https://foodeist.com/place/southern-charm-cafe-cape-canaveral-brevard-county) (4.7 rating across 1.3k+ reviews), a quick local breakfast five minutes from both parks. Confirm Thanksgiving-weekend hours before counting on it. Backup: **Juice N Java**, 75 N Orlando Ave, Cocoa Beach, opens [7:30 AM on Sundays](https://juicenjavacafe.com/).

**Manatee Sanctuary Park**, 701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral. Open 7 AM-dusk daily; admission and parking free. A short boardwalk and Banana River lookout; late November is the start of manatee season, not a guarantee of sightings. Forty-five unhurried minutes is plenty.

**Does not fit a pre-noon Sunday:** the Sands Space History Center's [Sunday hours start at noon](https://ccspacemuseum.org/location-and-hours), so it belongs to Saturday's itinerary, not this morning. An ordinary dawn-to-dusk visit to Space View Park is possible, but the 29-mile Titusville round trip only earns its place in Scenario A.

### Reference stops, late morning to evening

**Manatee Sanctuary Park**, 701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral. Boardwalk and Banana River lookout. Open 7 AM-dusk daily; admission and parking are free.

**Sands Space History Center**, 100 Space Port Way, Building 90328, Cape Canaveral. Its [posted Sunday hours are noon-4 PM](https://ccspacemuseum.org/location-and-hours), so it must follow Manatee rather than open the day. No base pass or scheduled tour is needed for this history center, and admission and parking are free. Allow 45 minutes; the museum inside the military base is a different stop.

**Cocoa Beach lunch (12:55-1:55):** Florida's Seafood Bar & Grill, 480 W Cocoa Beach Causeway, starts Sunday service at noon; Coconuts on the Beach, 2 Minutemen Causeway, is a more exposed downtown choice near the Thanksgiving-weekend art-show crowds. Pick one and keep a full hour for eating. Both serve meat; ask directly about vegetarian meals if relevant. Holiday hours and waits need a same-day check.

**Lori Wilson Park (2:05-3:20):** 1500 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach. Free beach access and parking, restrooms and showers; use the actual free county lot, not neighboring paid city lots. The late-November ocean can be cool and lifeguard service seasonal. Thanksgiving crowds may fill the lot, so allow time to park; Cherie Down Park in Cape Canaveral is a free-parking backup but changes the drive. Leaving by 3:20 allows roughly 45 minutes for the 29-mile northbound trip plus a small holiday buffer. Check live traffic before leaving.

**Early dinner (4:15-5:05): [Third Culture Kitchen](https://www.thirdculturekitchen.com/), 1000 Cheney Hwy, Titusville**, Sunday posted hours **11 AM-9 PM**. Brisket/chicken tacos and Korean fried chicken alongside vegan tofu coconut bowl, vegan burrito and avocado tacos. Eating before the viewing wait avoids a rushed, post-scrub, 8 PM restaurant queue. Ask the restaurant about Thanksgiving-weekend hours and whether they can seat you promptly; if delayed, take food to go and head to the riverfront. Do not count on bringing outside food into a venue without checking its rules.

Two other mixed-diet options: **[Indian Sizzler](https://indiansizzlerrestaurent.com/menu)**, 3520 S Washington Ave, serves chicken curry and a broad vegetarian menu (paneer, dal and vegetable biryani); [Sunday hours listed as 11 AM-9 PM](https://www.restaurantji.com/fl/titusville/indian-sizzler-/). It is farther south, so allow around 10 extra minutes to get back to Space View Park. **[Cafe Paradiso](https://thecafeparadiso.com/)** has meat and meatless dishes but is **Sunday brunch only until 3 PM**, not a Sunday dinner choice. None of these has a verified Thanksgiving-weekend special-hours schedule yet; call before committing to dinner.

### Reference: after-dark launch watch at Space View Park

The memorials are in the riverfront park, not a separate ticketed attraction. The after-dark launch watch requires **confirmed launch-specific access**: the [launch-viewing guide says dawn to dusk, open for launches](https://www.spacelaunchschedule.com/viewing-locations/space-view-park/), while ordinary park hours are daylight. Bring a layer, a compact chair or blanket, water, a charged phone/power bank, and a dim red flashlight; keep the waterfront clear and carry out trash. Download the webcast or live countdown before arrival, but expect patchy cell service in a crowd. The rocket may scrub after everyone has waited; expect slow roads after either outcome.

**If the launch slips or after-dark park access is not confirmed:** do not wait in Titusville for a fictitious 7 PM liftoff. Keep the two-hour beach block until about 4:05, drive north in time to visit the park **before its sunset close** only if traffic allows, then have a relaxed Titusville dinner at Third Culture or Indian Sizzler. If the shift is announced earlier, switch to the launch's actual date/time. If launch-viewing access is blocked but the launch remains on, check the city's designated open shoreline spots and parking rules before choosing one.
