# Cape Canaveral and Titusville: Sunday launch-watch day (November 29, 2026)

A free-attractions follow-up to the [Saturday Cape Canaveral day](https://github.com/peshzero/filedrop/blob/main/cape-canaveral-day/cape-canaveral-day.md), starting at 11 AM. Admission and parking at the named stops are free; meals cost extra. **The Falcon Heavy launch is not confirmed for Sunday at 7 PM.** Some launch trackers show Griffin Mission One at **November 29, 7:00 PM EST from LC-39A**, but that is the local-time conversion of a November 30, 00:00 UTC database date flagged as *approximate*; [Next Spaceflight](https://nextspaceflight.com/launches/details/6759/) says only **NET November 2026**, and the [payload owner's manifest](https://www.astrobotic.com/lunar-delivery/manifest/) says **2026**. Treat this as a **conditional launch-watch plan**, not a ticket to a guaranteed night launch. Check [SpaceX updates](https://www.spacex.com/launches/) and a [live launch tracker](https://nextspaceflight.com/launches/details/6759/) the night before and again before driving north. If a real window is announced, rebuild the times around it.

Sunset is **5:25 PM EST** on November 29 ([Cape Canaveral sun table](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/usa/cape-canaveral?month=11)), so a genuine 7 PM liftoff would be well after dark. Space View Park is ordinarily dawn-to-dusk but is [listed as open for launches](https://www.spacelaunchschedule.com/viewing-locations/space-view-park/). Confirm special launch access with the [City of Titusville](https://www.titusville.com/646/Space-View-Park) before relying on an after-dark visit. Do not enter a closed park.

If traffic or parking is worse than expected, use a legal open public shoreline viewing area rather than paying for a lot or parking illegally; [the city says shoreline viewing is possible and parking is limited but free](https://www.titusville.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=319).

## Late morning: reverse Saturday's two stops

**Manatee Sanctuary Park**, 701 Thurm Blvd, Cape Canaveral. Start at 11:00 with a boardwalk and Banana River lookout. Open 7 AM-dusk daily; admission and parking are free. Late November is the start of manatee season, not a guarantee of sightings.

**Sands Space History Center**, 100 Space Port Way, Building 90328, Cape Canaveral. Its [posted Sunday hours are noon-4 PM](https://ccspacemuseum.org/location-and-hours), so it must follow Manatee rather than open the day. No base pass or scheduled tour is needed for this history center, and admission and parking are free. Allow 45 minutes for its launch-complex exhibits; the museum inside the military base is a different stop.

## Lunch, beach, and dinner

**Cocoa Beach lunch (12:55-1:55):** Florida's Seafood Bar & Grill, 480 W Cocoa Beach Causeway, starts Sunday service at noon; Coconuts on the Beach, 2 Minutemen Causeway, is a more exposed downtown choice near the Thanksgiving-weekend art-show crowds. Pick one and keep a full hour for eating. Both serve meat; ask directly about vegetarian meals if relevant. Holiday hours and waits need a same-day check.

**Lori Wilson Park (2:05-3:20):** 1500 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach. Free beach access and parking, restrooms and showers; use the actual free county lot, not neighboring paid city lots. The late-November ocean can be cool and lifeguard service seasonal. Thanksgiving crowds may fill the lot, so allow time to park; Cherie Down Park in Cape Canaveral is a free-parking backup but changes the drive. Leaving by 3:20 allows roughly 45 minutes for the 29-mile northbound trip plus a small holiday buffer. Check live traffic before leaving.

**Early dinner (4:15-5:05): [Third Culture Kitchen](https://www.thirdculturekitchen.com/), 1000 Cheney Hwy, Titusville**, Sunday posted hours **11 AM-9 PM**. Brisket/chicken tacos and Korean fried chicken alongside vegan tofu coconut bowl, vegan burrito and avocado tacos. Eating before the viewing wait avoids a rushed, post-scrub, 8 PM restaurant queue. Ask the restaurant about Thanksgiving-weekend hours and whether they can seat you promptly; if delayed, take food to go and head to the riverfront. Do not count on bringing outside food into a venue without checking its rules.

Two other Saturday-list mixed-diet options: **[Indian Sizzler](https://indiansizzlerrestaurent.com/menu)**, 3520 S Washington Ave, serves chicken curry and a broad vegetarian menu (paneer, dal and vegetable biryani); [Sunday hours listed as 11 AM-9 PM](https://www.restaurantji.com/fl/titusville/indian-sizzler-/). It is farther south, so allow around 10 extra minutes to get back to Space View Park. **[Cafe Paradiso](https://thecafeparadiso.com/)** has meat and meatless dishes but is **Sunday brunch only until 3 PM**, not a Sunday dinner choice. None of these has a verified Thanksgiving-weekend special-hours schedule yet; call before committing to dinner.

## Launch watch at Space View Park

**Space View Park / U.S. Space Walk of Fame**, 8 Broad St, Titusville. The memorials are in the riverfront park, not a separate ticketed attraction. The [city recommends it for launch watching](https://www.titusville.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=319), with unobstructed shoreline, restrooms and limited free parking. The short stay before sundown is good for walking the memorials. The after-dark launch watch requires **confirmed launch-specific access**: the [launch-viewing guide says dawn to dusk, open for launches](https://www.spacelaunchschedule.com/viewing-locations/space-view-park/), while ordinary park hours are daylight. Bring a layer, a compact chair or blanket, water, a charged phone/power bank, and a dim red flashlight; keep the waterfront clear and carry out trash. Download the webcast or live countdown before arrival, but expect patchy cell service in a crowd. The rocket may scrub after everyone has waited; expect slow roads after either outcome.

**If the launch slips or after-dark park access is not confirmed:** do not wait in Titusville for a fictitious 7 PM liftoff. Keep the two-hour beach block until about 4:05, drive north in time to visit the park **before its sunset close** only if traffic allows, then have a relaxed Titusville dinner at Third Culture or Indian Sizzler. If the shift is announced earlier, switch to the launch's actual date/time; this Sunday file does not promise a launch. If launch-viewing access is blocked but the launch remains on, check the city's designated open shoreline spots and parking rules before choosing one.
